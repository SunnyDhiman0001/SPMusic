# Privacy Policy for SPMusic

**Effective date:** November 5, 2026

SPMusic ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline music player for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

**In short: SPMusic does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell your personal data.** Everything stays on your device.

## 1. Information we do not collect

SPMusic does not:

- Require an account or sign-in
- Collect your name, email, phone number, or location
- Use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services
- Show ads or use advertising IDs
- Send your music, listening history, or any other data to us or to third parties

## 2. Information the app accesses on your device

### Audio files (media access)
To show and play your music, the app asks for permission to read audio files on your device (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` on Android 13+, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on older versions). The app reads song details such as title, artist, album, duration, and artwork through Android's MediaStore.

This information is used only to display and play your music inside the app. It is never uploaded or shared.

### Data saved locally
The app saves the following on your device only, using Android's local storage:

- Your favorite songs
- Your recently played songs
- Your playlists
- Your app settings, such as dark or light theme

This data stays on your device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## 3. Other permissions

| Permission | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Foreground service / media playback | Keep music playing when the app is in the background, with notification and lock-screen controls |
| Wake lock | Prevent playback from stopping when the screen is off |

## 4. Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It is built with open-source libraries (for example Flutter, just_audio, and permission_handler) that run entirely on your device.

## 5. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data, because we do not collect any.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > SPMusic > Storage, or uninstall the app. Your original audio files are never modified or deleted by the app.

## 7. Your choices

You can revoke the media permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > SPMusic > Permissions. Without it, the app cannot list or play your songs.

## 8. Children's privacy

SPMusic does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 9. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Please keep your device secure and up to date.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
